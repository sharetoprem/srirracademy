Our Story

Sri RR Academy was founded in 2001 by a group of passionate friends who shared a common vision - to provide quality education and help students achieve their dreams of securing government jobs.

What started as a small coaching center with just 10 students has now grown into one of the most trusted and successful competitive exam coaching institutes in Tamil Nadu.

Our name "RR" stands for "Read and Rule" - a philosophy that embodies our commitment to empowering students through knowledge, enabling them to rule their own futures by securing prestigious government positions.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality coaching for government competitive exams and ensure every student gets personalized attention and guidance to achieve their career goals. We believe in "Pay Once - Study Lifetime" model that makes quality education accessible to all.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and results-oriented coaching institute in South India, producing 1000+ government officers annually and contributing to nation-building by creating competent, ethical, and dedicated public servants.
