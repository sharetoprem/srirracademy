About Sri RR Academy

Started in 2001 by a group of passionate friends with a vision to provide quality education for government job aspirants, Sri RR Academy has grown to become one of the most trusted coaching institutes in Tamil Nadu.

Our journey began with just 10 students and a dream. Today, we are proud to have 250+ successful placements in various government departments including TNPSC, RRB, Banking, Police, and more.

We believe in the RR Family concept - once you join us, you become part of our family forever. Our 100% placement assistance and lifetime support ensure that every student achieves their dream job.
